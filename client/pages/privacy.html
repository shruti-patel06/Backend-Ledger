Backend Ledger

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Backend Ledger is a personal portfolio project demonstrating a double-entry ledger. It is not a real bank and holds no real money.

What we store

  • Your name and email address, used to log in and identify your account.
  • Your password, stored only as a one-way bcrypt hash.
  • Your ledger accounts and the demo transactions you make.

How it is used

Your email address is used to send a welcome email when you register and a notification when you make a transfer. These emails are sent through Gmail using the Gmail API, which is used only to send these notifications — no mailbox contents are read or stored.

Sharing

Your data is not sold or shared with anyone. It is stored in MongoDB Atlas and processed by the app's hosting providers (Render and Vercel) only to run the service.

Deleting your data

To have your account and data deleted, open an issue on the project's GitHub repository.
